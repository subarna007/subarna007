### Research projects

| Project | What it shows |
|---|---|
| [**Glacial lake mapping with Sentinel-2**](https://github.com/subarna007/glacial-lake-mapping-sentinel2) | U-Net trained on the Hi-MAG inventory and tested on held-out strips of the Himalaya: pixel IoU 0.669 vs 0.490 for NDWI, 70.6% vs 45.9% of lakes found. Applied to the Lende / upper Bhotekoshi area of the 2025 and 2026 floods, 2017-2024. [Paper (PDF)](https://github.com/subarna007/glacial-lake-mapping-sentinel2/blob/main/paper/Dahal_2026_glacial_lake_mapping.pdf) |
| [**Skin lesion classification on ISIC 2018**](https://github.com/subarna007/skin-lesion-classification-isic2018) | Seven-class dermoscopy classifier evaluated on a lesion-grouped split. ConvNeXt-Tiny with class-balanced sampling raised melanoma sensitivity from 62.2% to 82.1% over a ResNet-50 baseline, and a screening threshold fixed on validation data caught 94.2% of test melanomas. Includes Grad-CAM, similar-case retrieval and a triage web app. [Paper (PDF)](https://github.com/subarna007/skin-lesion-classification-isic2018/blob/main/paper/Dahal_2026_skin_lesion_classification.pdf) |
| [**Fine-tuning vs. LLMs for Nepali sentiment**](https://github.com/subarna007/Nepali-Sentiment-finetune-vs-llm) | Compares a TF-IDF baseline, fine-tuned XLM-RoBERTa and Qwen2.5-3B (zero- and few-shot) on 33.5k Nepali tweets. Fine-tuning reached 0.707 macro-F1; the untrained LLM reached 0.480 and was beaten by the simple baseline. [Paper (PDF)](https://github.com/subarna007/Nepali-Sentiment-finetune-vs-llm/blob/main/paper/Dahal_2026_nepali_sentiment_finetune_vs_llm.pdf) |
